# Policies

* [AI covenant](ai-covenant.md) - The rules for AI assistance in this repository: the person who submits a change is its author, FINOS's files stay FINOS's, an element says what CALM's files say, and AI co-authorship in commits is discouraged.
* [Licence and attribution](license-and-attribution.md) - Which licence applies where: this project's files under Apache 2.0, FINOS's tree and CALM under FINOS's Apache 2.0 terms, CALM's descriptions kept word for word, and the bundle under CC BY 4.0.
* [Security policy](security-policy.md) - How to report a vulnerability privately, what this repository releases, and what holds each surface that executes here: the workflows, the scheduled librarian and the release.
